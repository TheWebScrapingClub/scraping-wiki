# FreeSocks

## What it is

FreeSocks is a VPN designed for individuals whose internet access is censored, functioning as a privacy-respecting VPN in other regions. It aims to provide internet freedom and privacy for users in heavily-censored areas.

## How it works

To get started, users must solve a quick human check to receive a 32-digit account number, which is used for signing back in. This account number is how users sign in, and no email or password is collected. Subscriptions provide access to an Xray-powered VLESS subscription URL, which can be pasted into compatible VPN clients.
