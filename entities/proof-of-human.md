# Proof of Human

## What it is

Proof of Human is a detection system designed to classify web sessions as either human or automated. It is used in bot detection benchmarks to evaluate the ability of various adversaries, including AI agents and stealth browsers, to bypass detection mechanisms.

## How it works

Proof of Human utilizes a machine-learning model to classify sessions. This classification is based on a combination of behavioral signals and device signals. These signals include user interactions such as typing, mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling.
