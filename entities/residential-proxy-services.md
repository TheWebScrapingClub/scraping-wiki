# Residential Proxy Services

## What it is

Residential proxy services allow customers to rent Internet addresses to anonymize their web traffic or spoof their true location. These services are a major source of unwanted and often malicious traffic, including account takeover activity, advertising fraud, and mass content scraping for AI projects.

## How it works

The XCPE botnet loaded user devices with software that made their Internet address available for resale by these residential proxy services. This mechanism allows customers to rent these addresses to mask their identity or location when performing online activities.

This resale mechanism was exploited by criminal affiliates who loaded malware modules onto infected devices, enabling the botnet to operate and monetize the compromised internet addresses.
