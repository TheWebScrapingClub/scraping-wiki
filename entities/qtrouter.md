# QTRouter

## What it is

QTRouter is a tool identified by the FBI as part of a network of proxy devices used by a Chinese state-sponsored hacking group. The company that developed QTRouter allegedly provided its customers with access to botnets of hacked internet-of-things (IoT) devices and commercial proxy services.

## How it works

QTRouter's service allegedly managed customers’ access to a botnet network of infected IoT devices. Additionally, it managed access to a network of commercial proxies, known as virtual private servers, which could be rented and utilized in hacking campaigns.
