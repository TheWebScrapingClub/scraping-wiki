# VNC2Go

## What it is

VNC2Go is described as a private, in-browser service that routes traffic through a random proxy. It operates in a private mode, emphasizing user privacy.

## How it works

The service routes traffic through a random proxy. Sessions utilized by the service are limited to 10 minutes.

## Known limitations

Sessions are limited to 10m.
