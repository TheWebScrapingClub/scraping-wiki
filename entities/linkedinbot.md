# LinkedInBot

## What it is

The LinkedInBot is a scraper utilized by the social network LinkedIn to gather information regarding URLs that are shared within posts or messages, such as displaying a link preview.

## How it works

The bot operates using a specific user-agent, which as of April 2024 is `LinkedInBot/1.0 (compatible; Mozilla/5.0; Apache-HttpClient +http://www.linkedin.com)`. It functions by operating from IP addresses that belong to the LinkedIn Corporation autonomous system (AS).
