# Device and Browser Info

## What it is

Device and Browser Info is a platform offering tools and articles for browser fingerprinting and bot detection. It provides a suite of features including browser fingerprinting, user agent information, HTTP headers, proxy/TOR IP addresses, disposable email and phone numbers, and more.

## How it works

Device and Browser Info offers a variety of tools and articles to help with browser fingerprinting and bot detection. It includes a page to test whether a bot is detected based solely on fingerprinting signals, a searchable UI for temporary phone numbers, and improvements to fingerprint stability on Chrome. The platform also provides a way to see your browser fingerprint, lists of disposable email and phone numbers, and proxy IP addresses.
