# Residential proxy networks

## What it is

Residential proxy networks route internet traffic through real consumer IP addresses. These networks are commonly utilized for activities such as web scraping, market research, and ad verification. They have attracted scrutiny due to the potential for abuse, where they can be used to conceal malicious activity or provide access to networks through consumer devices.

## How it works

These networks operate by routing internet traffic through genuine consumer IP addresses. Many applications obtain user consent through a one-time prompt during setup, allowing users to choose between viewing advertisements or enabling the application to monetize their internet connection via the proxy network.

## Known limitations

Proxy-enabled TV apps pose security risks because they operate on the same local networks as routers, printers, cameras, NAS devices, and computers. If the safeguards of the proxy providers fail or are bypassed, attackers could potentially gain a foothold inside home networks.
