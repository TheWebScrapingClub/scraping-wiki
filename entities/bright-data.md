# Bright Data

## What it is

Bright Data is a residential proxy network identified in research concerning proxy software development kits (SDKs) embedded in smart TV applications. The company provides residential proxy services that allow developers to turn a user's device into a residential proxy node.

## How it works

Bright Data provides residential proxy networks that are bundled into proxy SDKs used by applications on smart TVs. These services are rented to paying customers who wish to use their device as a proxy node.

## TWSC experience

Not yet tested by TWSC.

## Known limitations

The proxy companies, including Bright Data, incorporate technological countermeasures to prevent proxy service customers from being able to interact with and control other devices on the proxy user’s local network.
