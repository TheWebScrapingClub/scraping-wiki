# Interviews archive

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-02-20 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/interviews-archive-web-scraping-key-people?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

This page is the index of our interview series with people who shape the web scraping industry. It matters if you want to hear from the people behind the tools and services you use every day, and not only read about the tools themselves. Proxies, scraping platforms and reverse engineering are all part of your daily work, and the people running them see the market from a different angle than we do as users.

There are five interviews so far, all published about four years ago. We talked with Neha Setia, developer advocate at Zyte, Neil Emeigh, CEO at Rayobyte, Ondra Urban from Apify, and Martin Ganchev, VP of Enterprise Partnerships at Smartproxy. The fifth is with Veritas, who works on deobfuscation, the anti obfuscation master. Each entry links to the full conversation, where I ask about background, how they got into the industry and how they see it.

## What's inside

- Interview #1 is with Neha Setia of Zyte, who runs workshops for system integrators and clients and looks for AI and ML use cases.
- Interviews #2 and #4 cover the proxy side, with Neil Emeigh as CEO of Rayobyte and Martin Ganchev on enterprise partnerships at Smartproxy.
- Interview #3 features Ondra Urban from Apify, a scraping platform view that complements the proxy providers.
- Interview #5 with Veritas is the technical one, focused on deobfuscation.
