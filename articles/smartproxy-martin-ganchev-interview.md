# Interview #4: Martin Ganchev - Smartproxy

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-01-08 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/smartproxy-martin-ganchev-interview?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Proxy providers sit at the base of almost every scraping stack, yet few of us know how they get their IPs, how they check quality, or why a proxy company ends up selling scraping APIs. For anyone choosing or combining providers, those answers matter more than a feature list.

For this monthly interview I talked with Martin Ganchev, VP of Enterprise Partnerships at Smartproxy. We covered how the company moved from self-service residential and datacenter proxies to a scraping infrastructure business, and how the proxy market works from the inside. We also went through how IPs are procured and tested, what a customer should check when picking a provider, and the current API lineup. I also brought him a market research figure on in-house scraping versus bought datasets, and asked where scraping tools are heading. It ends with a story from his first day at the company.

## What's inside

- How Smartproxy sources exit nodes, including wholesalers and the consent requirement for end users' devices.
- The quality checks applied to IPs: stability, speed, location in databases, stress testing and an internal selection algorithm.
- Why Martin says large clients often use several proxy providers, and how he ties price to reputation and quality.
- His view on why big retailers scrape in-house, why smaller companies buy datasets, and why some do both.
