# Two years of The Web Scraping Club

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2024-08-11 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/two-years-of-the-web-scraping-club?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

The first issue of The Web Scraping Club went out on August 2, 2022, to two people. After 221 posts the audience grew to over 1500 subscribers per year and stands at 3300+ today. It is still a niche newsletter, but retention is high, so I think we are doing something right. I'm not a professional technical writer and not the most organized person around, and some issues show it.

For the two-year mark I ran three polls to know the audience better, since many of you joined in the past year. They cover scraping experience, the kind of web data you work with, and profession. I also write about the problem of publishing two articles per week without giving up quality, the new video interview series on YouTube, and my August break.

## What's inside

- What each poll is for: experience shapes the content for the next 12 months, data type shapes the examples, and profession sets how much space open-source and commercial solutions get.
- The plan to build a more structured writing method, aimed at better quality without cutting the number of posts.
- The first five video interviews are already recorded, with guests from Kasada, Datadome, Scrapoxy, Databoutique.com and Bright Data, and they will be released every two weeks.
- How the paid plan covers video postproduction and keeps product tests free of sponsors, plus how the holiday slows down the usual calendar of The Lab.

**In the full article:** The poll forms, the guest list and release schedule of the video interviews, and the holiday dates with the contact address for subscribers.
