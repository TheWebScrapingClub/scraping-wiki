# Tik Tok Scraping: how to do it properly

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-07-16 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/tik-tok-scraping-how-to-do-it-properly?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

TikTok has more than 1 billion monthly users and exposes metrics such as views, followers and engagement rate. That makes it a target for hashtag tracking, engagement analysis, content extraction, follower growth monitoring and trend prediction. The catch is that the first excitement fades quickly: rate limits, geo-blocks and the risk of getting blocked show up, and privacy rules in Europe make collecting user data a legal problem.

I walk through the main scraping use cases on TikTok and a set of best practices for staying under the radar and out of trouble. Then I lay out a workflow, from defining the data you need to analyzing it once collected. On collection, I list three routes: building your own scraper, buying a ready-made one, or applying for the official API. I also cover where proxies fit in and what to check in a provider.

## What's inside

- Why I suggest scraping market trends and post engagement rather than user data, given privacy laws in Europe and elsewhere.
- How to pace requests: imitating human behavior with delays and batching, and tracking success rates, errors and response times.
- What each collection route requires, including the developer account and access application for the official TikTok API.
- How to treat the data afterward: removing irrelevant or redundant records, running NLP for sentiment and topics, and comparing with external sources.
