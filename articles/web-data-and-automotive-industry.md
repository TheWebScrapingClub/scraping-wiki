# Web data and automotive industry

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2025-03-09 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/web-data-and-automotive-industry?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

European automakers are under pressure. Factories are closing, the EV transition is slower than planned, EVs are still too expensive for most buyers, charging infrastructure is uneven, and Chinese brands are entering with affordable products. Analysts and managers have a lot to untangle, and web data can add pieces to the puzzle.

We go through five use cases, with examples from the Italian market that work in any country. We look at manufacturer websites for vehicle availability, cross-country prices and dealer networks, using Tesla and BYD as examples. We look at used and new car listings such as AutoScout24 for pricing and demand. We look at Italy's national EV charging platform, PUN, for infrastructure trends. We look at UNRAE registration statistics, which are published as PDFs. Finally, we look at scraping new car prices. For each one we cover what to collect and what the data can tell you.

## What's inside

- Which fields to extract from car listings (make, model, year, mileage, fuel type, location, seller type) and why listing quality makes version-to-version comparison hard.
- How scraping PUN regularly can show charger growth, regional gaps, capacity shifts and operator market share, and who can use that data.
- How to find UNRAE's PDF reports through the website's sitemap, and which extraction options we mention for the tables inside them.
- Which market trends UNRAE data can reveal, from fuel type shifts and SUV share to top models and regional differences.

**In the full article:** The links to the Tesla dataset and the sources cited, the BYD dealer map, the full attribute lists for listings and EV chargers, and the UNRAE trend examples with their figures.
