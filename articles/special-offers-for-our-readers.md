Running scraping projects means paying for the same few things over and over: proxies, an unblocking or scraping API, and sometimes an anti-detect browser. Prices and trial terms change often, and most providers hand out better conditions through partner channels than on their public pricing pages.

Club Deals is where I keep the offers our partners give to Web Scraping Club subscribers. The page is split by partner tier: one Platinum partner, a Gold group, and a longer Silver list. The Silver list is organized by what you buy: proxy solutions, API and scraping infrastructure, and anti-detect browsers. Offers differ by provider. Some are percentage discounts on a first purchase or a plan, others are free trials, free traffic or credits, or a matched first deposit. It is a reference page that we update as partners change their terms, not an article to read start to finish.
