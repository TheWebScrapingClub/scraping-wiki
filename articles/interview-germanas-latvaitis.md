# Interview #10 - Germanas Latvaitis

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-07-02 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/interview-germanas-latvaitis?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Proxies are a given in web scraping, and the more we rely on them, the less we ask what it takes to run a proxy network. Mobile proxies make this clearer. They cost more, they are sourced differently from datacenter IPs, and they raise questions about where the IPs come from and who agreed to share them. These are the points I care about, and so do our customers.

For the monthly interview, I talked with Germanas Latvaitis, Head of Product at Smartproxy. I asked him about the main challenges of running a proxy infrastructure and what changes when a provider launches a mobile service. We went through how mobile IPs are acquired, how bandwidth costs shape pricing, and what standards partners must meet for ethical sourcing. We also covered compliance, the most common mobile proxy use cases, and how someone could become a supplier. The interview ends with a lighter question about his early days.

## What's inside

- Why Scraping APIs were introduced as an out-of-the-box answer to users getting their IPs blocked
- How the mobile service reuses the residential backend, and the detection problem that creates
- How Smartproxy handles KYC, blocked target categories and monitoring of customer activity
- What he says about reselling options and wholesale prices for people entering the proxy business
