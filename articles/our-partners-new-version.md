# Our Partners (New Version)

Companies in the scraping industry often ask how a partnership with The Web Scraping Club works and what they get at each level. I keep one page that answers that, so nobody has to guess what a placement looks like or how much space it has.

This is the new version of that page. We work with three tiers. Platinum is a banner with a company description, an offer for the people who follow us and an affiliate link, and only one partner holds it each month. Gold is a short text listing for three companies, covering the company, its different services and an offer. Silver is a shorter listing for three more. Gold and Silver entries are in alphabetical order. The entries in this version are placeholders that show the layout and the character limits.

## What's inside

- The Platinum banner has a maximum size of 1000x500 px and about 250 characters for the description and the offer.
- Gold listings get about 200 characters, with a maximum of three lines of total text.
- Silver listings get about 100 characters for a short description and an offer.
