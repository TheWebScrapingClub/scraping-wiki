# The Web Scraping Club in 2026

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2025-12-31 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/the-web-scraping-club-in-2026?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

The posting frequency of this newsletter dropped by the end of October 2025. My family has grown and my workload peaked, so 24 hours a day were not enough. The equilibrium is back, and I wanted to tell the 6500+ subscribers what to expect from 2026 before the year ends.

I lay out the roadmap in three parts. On tools, I cover where the Proxy Price Benchmark stands and what comes next, starting with ScrapeAdvisor, a benchmark for families of scraping tools, from web unblockers to scraping APIs. On content, we keep the usual mix of anti-bot bypassing, scraping techniques and AI applied to data collection and quality. On video, I talk about the YouTube channel and the interview series I want to bring back.

## What's inside

- Why the pace slowed, and the thanks to Antonello Zanini and Federico Trotta for covering the months when I could not write as much.
- The Proxy Price Benchmark needs a small team handling data management, so the prices stay more up to date before new tools follow.
- ScrapeAdvisor is currently on hold, and other tools are planned for the second half of 2026.
- The YouTube channel has not taken off yet, and I ask which guests you would like to see in the interview series.

**In the full article:** Our timeline for ScrapeAdvisor and the later 2026 tools, the plan for the Proxy Price Benchmark data team, and the open call for interview guests.
