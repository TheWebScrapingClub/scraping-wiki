# Hands On Episodes

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-03-07 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/hands-on-episodes?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Before you pay for a web scraping product, you want to know what it does on real targets. Vendor pages and demos rarely tell you that, and a trial costs time as well as money. That is why I started the Hands On series, where I try out software and solutions from the major web scraping companies and write a sort of review.

This page is the index of the series. It lists every Hands On issue in one place, so you can find the product you are evaluating. At the moment it points to two episodes. Hands On #1 tests the Bright Data Web Unblocker proxy. Hands On #2 tests the new Zyte API. Each episode is a separate post with its own tests, and the index links to both. If you want a product reviewed, you can write to me.

## What's inside

- The series is meant to help you evaluate a product before spending money and time on your own tests.
- Hands On #1 is about Bright Data Web Unblocker, a proxy-based unblocking product.
- Hands On #2 covers the new Zyte API.
- The list is organized by episode number, and each entry links to the full post.
