# Scraping Insights - A video interview series by The Web Scraping Club - Join us

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2024-07-17 (for paid subscribers). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/scraping-insights-a-video-interview?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

I like webinars and video interviews, and I watch a lot of YouTube, but too often I finish one without knowing even 1% more than before. The ones worth the time feature people with real expertise and a different point of view. I wanted a series built only on those, with guests from different backgrounds who help us understand several aspects of the web scraping industry.

I start recording tomorrow, and the videos go on the new Web Scraping Club YouTube channel from September. The first two interviews cover the anti-bot industry, browser fingerprinting and other techniques, and good bots versus bad bots, with Nick Rieniets (CTO of Kasada) and Antoine Vastel (VP of research at Datadome). Then we reverse the view with Fabien Vauchelles (Wiremind, creator of Scrapoxy), since anti-bots changed over time and our scrapers have to adapt.

## What's inside

- These are not "how to bypass X, Y, or Z" tutorials. I may make some in the future, but in a proper format and not in this series.
- Nothing here is marketing material: I have nothing to sell and neither do the guests.
- Don't expect Nick and Antoine to reveal their secret recipe, but the conversations should still be worth watching.
- Later episodes bring CEOs of well-known companies and web data experts, with schedules still being worked out.
