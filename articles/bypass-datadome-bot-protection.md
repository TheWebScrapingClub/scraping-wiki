Datadome is not something you beat with a single trick. Over the years I have written about it from several angles, and those posts ended up scattered across the archive. This page collects the Datadome-related ones in one place, so you can pick the technique that fits your target.

The list covers four posts. One looks at the rise of antidetect browsers and what they mean for scraping. THE LAB #11 covers the anti-detect anti-bot matrix. THE LAB #8 uses Bezier curves to produce human-like mouse movements. THE LAB #2 is a real-world case of scraping a website protected by Datadome that also relies on xsrf tokens. The LAB posts come with code and an explanation of the methodology used.

## What's inside

- The antidetect browser post is the background piece, on why this class of tools appeared and how it relates to scraping.
- The Bezier curve post deals with the behavioral side of detection, meaning how the mouse moves rather than which browser you run.
- The THE LAB #2 case combines Datadome with xsrf tokens on a real site, so the token handling matters as much as the anti-bot layer.
- THE LAB is a series of real-world use cases with code, and #2 was one of its first entries.
