# From Scripts to Agents: The Evolving Career For Web Scraping Professionals

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2025-10-05 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/the-evolving-career-scrapers?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Web scraping work looked very different in the early 2000s. Most of it was freelance gigs on platforms like Upwork. Today the market is wider, anti-bot defenses are more sophisticated, and AI is entering the game. If you are just starting, that can look scary. If you are established, it still pays to stop and check where the profession is heading, so you have a north star for your own growth.

I went through how the career has changed over about 20 years, in four parts. First, how people make money: code, data, consultancy, or a job at a scraping company. Second, the skills around the scraper, from proxies and browser automation to anti-bot systems, anti-detect browsers and infrastructure. Third, the industries that pay for data extraction, including e-commerce, financial markets and social media. Last, what AI and LLMs mean for the next years of the job.

## What's inside

- Selling pre-built scrapers in a marketplace such as the Apify store versus building custom solutions, and how a tool like Lovable lowers the effort of a micro SaaS
- Why picking the right proxy type, from residential to mobile, saves money and can decide whether a project gets finished
- Paid use cases beyond price comparison, such as spotting limited-edition sneaker releases or concert tickets in near real time
- Three AI angles: scraping for AI training data, using LLMs inside pipelines, and scraping from chatbots
