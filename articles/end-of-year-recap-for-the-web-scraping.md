# End of year recap for The Web Scraping Club

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-12-31 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/end-of-year-recap-for-the-web-scraping?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

This is the last post of 2023, the point where we look back at the year and plan the next one. I started The Web Scraping Club because after 10+ years in the industry I felt web scraping experts needed a place to openly share tools, techniques and experiences, especially now that scraping keeps getting harder. The community grew a lot this year, on the newsletter and on our Discord server.

I go through the numbers and the three most-read posts of the year. Two of them are about AI and LLMs, the third is a Cloudflare bypass piece from The Lab. Then I lay out the 2024 plan. The goal is to move the club away from a one-man-band show, with more guest posts from key people in the industry, a monthly legal column, and a change in how often The Lab comes out. I also explain what stays free, what is paywalled and why, and how to get a free plan.
