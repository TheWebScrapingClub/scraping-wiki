# What we've learnt from the Nimble AI Month

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-07-01 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/ai-and-webscraping-lesson-learned?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

A month of articles on AI and web scraping, run with Nimble, has ended, and the contest closed it out with many submitted solutions. The question behind the whole series was practical: where does AI actually touch our work today, and does it change what a scraping operator needs to know? That matters because the answer decides which skills and tools are worth your time.

I close the month with three things I learned personally. The first is that AI is already in use, both against anti-bot challenges and for HTML parsing, in products like the Nimble Browser that anyone can use. The second is that these solutions work on the most common websites, and a general parsing model for the whole web looks possible in the coming years. The third is that web scraping stays needed, because AI models keep asking for more training data. I also cover what comes next: a month on proxies, with an interview on how a proxy vendor works inside.

## What's inside

- How the contest ended, when Nimble contacts the winners, and what participants get from me
- Why growing demand for AI training data keeps scraping relevant, and why operators should learn the new tools
- The limit of current AI parsing: it works well on common websites, not yet on the whole web
- The proxy series ahead: a vendor interview, a scraping case on a site protected by Akamai, and TikTok trends with mobile proxies
