# Consulting

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2024-09-16 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/consulting?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

A web data pipeline under pressure usually shows it in one of three ways: high costs, technical hurdles or scaling challenges. Blocks eat time when the fix is trial and error, proxy bills grow if nobody checks them, and scaling collection without a plan is guesswork.

I co-founded Data Boutique and RE Analytics, and since 2009 we have worked on web scraping for some of the world's largest companies. This page presents our consulting work: unbiased, confidential advice tailored to your case. It rests on three objectives. We help you solve blocks by choosing the right tools and techniques, reduce costs through a detailed infrastructure review, and build a scraping strategy that supports your project's growth. The way in is an assessment questionnaire.

## What's inside

- Solving blocks means matching tools and techniques to the target instead of testing at random.
- Cost work centers on proxy spending and a review of the whole infrastructure, with savings of up to 90% claimed.
- The strategy objective ties data collection to how the project is expected to grow.
- The advice is described as confidential and unbiased, and it starts from an assessment questionnaire.
