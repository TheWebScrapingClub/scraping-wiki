# Advertise on The Web Scraping Club

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2025-12-28 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/advertise-on-the-web-scraping-club?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

The Web Scraping Club is a niche publication for people who build, maintain, or buy scraping infrastructure. Most scraping tools are not bought on impulse. Teams evaluate them when something breaks, scale increases, blocks appear or costs become unsustainable, and that is when they read our posts on Cloudflare, Kasada and Turnstile, proxy setup, cost cutting and unblocker benchmarks. A sponsor placed there sits inside the decision context, not outside it.

I lay out how sponsorship works in 2026. There are two tiers, Platinum and Gold, and Gold is now sold per post instead of by subscription. I explain what we sell, which is contextual presence and repeated exposure to technical content, and what we do not sell: guaranteed clicks, impression targets, performance ads or editorial influence. I also describe who reads us and why limited inventory fits tools that teams evaluate over time.

## What's inside

- Platinum goes to one sponsor per month and is not tied to any single post. It is about owning the month.
- Gold is capped at two sponsors per post, down from three, and can be bought for a single post or as a multi-post bundle.
- Primary Gold sits right after the introduction and is reserved for longer-term bundles. Secondary Gold sits later, with lower visual weight, and suits single posts and short test campaigns.
- Readers come from Big Tech, financial services and hedge funds, and SaaS and data-driven marketplaces, with a strong US presence among paying readers.
