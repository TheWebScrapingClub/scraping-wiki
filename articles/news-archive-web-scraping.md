Web scraping news matters when it changes what we can run in production. A court ruling on public data is a good example: the earliest wrap-up in this archive opens with the Supreme Court of Korea saying that scraping publicly available data from a competitor's website is legal. I keep the monthly recaps so that kind of news does not get lost across a dozen sources.

This page is the index of those recaps, newest first. It runs from the February 2023 recap back through January 2023, the full 2022 year-end recap, and the November and October 2022 editions. It ends with the brief wrap-up that started the series. Each entry shows only a short preview and a link to the full post, so this page tells you what is there and where to find it.
