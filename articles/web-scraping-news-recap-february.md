# Web Scraping news recap - February 2023

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-02-26 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/web-scraping-news-recap-february?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Every month I collect what mattered in web scraping, and February 2023 had both legal and technical news. On the legal side, Meta went after two companies over scraping on Facebook. One case involves fake accounts used to collect personal data. The other is the older dispute over what counts as public data and what the Terms of Service forbid. Papers from that second case also showed a detail about the relationship between the two parties.

On the tech side, we look at two changes that affect how we build scrapers. Oxylabs released a web unblocker that promises to handle proxies, headers, cookies and fingerprints for you. Chrome's new headless mode is also coming into wider use, and Antoine Vastel of Datadome analyzed what it means for fingerprint-based detection. I also point to a video from an attorney on the legal side of scraping, and to the most read post of the month, our Anti-Detect Anti-Bot matrix.

## What's inside

- What Meta alleges against Voyager Labs, and why I think the Terms of Service argument is hard to dispute.
- The Meta and Bright Data case: the public data defense and an odd detail that surfaced in the court papers.
- How Oxylabs describes its AI-powered unblocker choosing proxies, headers, cookies and JavaScript fingerprints.
- Why the new headless Chrome fingerprint, much closer to headful Chrome, matters for detection based on browser fingerprinting.
