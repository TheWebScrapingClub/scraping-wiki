# What is web scraping?

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-02-07 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/what-is-webscraping?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Before talking about proxies, fingerprints or anti-bot systems, it helps to agree on the basics. Web scraping is the automatic collection of structured data from websites, and it replaces manual copy and paste, which is slow and full of human errors. For anyone who needs prices, listings, rankings or news at scale, it is the standard way to get data into a usable shape.

I wrote this as a plain introduction. I describe what scraping is and walk through the five steps of a typical project, from picking the target and inspecting the page to sending requests, parsing the HTML and storing the output. I list the main use cases, from market research and SEO monitoring to finance, content aggregation and real estate. I also cover the benefits, the legal and ethical limits, the tool families (Python libraries, browser extensions, no-code tools), a short set of best practices and where AI may take the field.
