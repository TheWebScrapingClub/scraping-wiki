# Scrapecon and The Web Scraping Club contest

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-10-16 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/scrapecon-and-the-web-scraping-club?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Scrapecon 2023, hosted by Bright Data, runs online on November 7 starting at 2 pm CET, with speakers from Kaggle to Rakuten. Alongside it, The Web Scraping Club is running a contest for people who build scraping tools and want a jury of practitioners to look at the logic and the tools behind their work.

We lay out how the contest works: who sits on the jury, which kinds of projects qualify, how entries are scored and what the submission asks for. Three projects go to a live panel at the event, where the jury discusses them and names a winner. Projects can be old or built for the occasion, but commercial products are out. Submissions close on October 30.

## What's inside

- The jury is Darío Kondratiuk, Alexander Afanasyev and Greg Gorlen, with experience in Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium and scraping in general.
- Eligible examples include packages or extensions for Scrapy, Puppeteer or Playwright, tools like fingerprint injectors or CAPTCHA solvers, and scrapers that get past well-known anti-bots.
- Entries are judged on technical execution, difficulty of the project and innovation, meaning how much it makes web scraping easier.
- The project must be hosted on GitHub, and you have to prove you own it. Every valid submission also gets a month of paid content from The Web Scraping Club.

**In the full article:** The submission form, the registration and ownership verification steps, the prize details, and the Discord and email contacts for questions.
