# Scrapecon 2023 - Win 2000$ with your web scraping skills

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-10-17 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/scrapecon-2023-win-2000-with-your?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

Scrapecon 2023, hosted by Bright Data, is an online event on November 7, starting at 2 pm CET. Inside it, together with The Web Scraping Club, we are running a contest for people who build web scraping tools. A jury of Darío Kondratiuk, Alexander Afanasyev and Greg Gorlen will review three projects live, and the winner takes $2,000 in Bright Data credits.

Here I explain how to enter. You register on the Scrapecon site, submit through a form, and host the project on GitHub. Submissions close on October 30. Qualifying projects include extensions for Scrapy, Puppeteer or Playwright, tools like fingerprint injectors and CAPTCHA solvers, and scrapers that bypass well-known anti-bots. Old projects count, and so do ones built for the contest. Commercial products don't. Three finalists are discussed in public and the winner is declared at the event.

## What's inside

- The three judging criteria are technical execution, difficulty of the project, and innovation, meaning how much the solution makes web scraping easier.
- Participants must prove they own the submitted project, and we contact them by email about a Bright Data account with a $50 deposit for use on the project.
- The jury's experience covers web scraping, Playwright, Puppeteer and Selenium, which hints at the kind of projects that fit.
- Questions go to our Discord server or to my email address.
