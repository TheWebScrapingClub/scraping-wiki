# The Web Scraping Club 2024 Wrap

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2024-12-22 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/the-web-scraping-club-2024-wrap?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

2024 was a year of growth for The Web Scraping Club. The audience more than doubled with zero advertising budget, only word of mouth and SEO, and paying subscribers grew too. The newsletter became a real company this year, with a VAT ID and the bookkeeping that comes with it. That means more accounting for me, but also more money to invest in tools and people.

Here I go through the numbers and the main costs of running TWSC. I explain the three phases where I use AI in writing, and I ran a few AI-detection tools on one of my own posts to see what they would say. I also own up to the goals I missed, like keeping the unblocker and anti-detect browser benchmarks updated. Then I look at 2025: more authors, more YouTube interviews, more tool tests, and the topics I expect to cover.

## What's inside

- Where the money goes: AI tools, social channels and SEO, scraping tools, bookkeeping and a video editor.
- Why AI helps with ideas, a first structure and grammar checks, but does not write the posts.
- I published 107 posts in 2024 and wrote 93 of them, so I am looking for guest authors with a strong scraping background.
- The YouTube channel started with five interviews. The 2025 target is at least 50 videos, with guests from the anti-bot industry, AI companies and heavy users of web data.

**In the full article:** Subscriber growth figures, the complete expense list, the output of each AI-detection test, the details of the missed objectives and the contact details for authors and interview guests.
