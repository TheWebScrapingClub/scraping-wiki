# Data collection challenge with Nimble

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2023-06-24 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/nimble-data-collection-challenge?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

We are running a web scraping challenge together with Nimble, one of our partners. It is a practical test of data collection skills, open to experienced data analysts, budding data scientists and anyone with a technical interest in the field. For participants it is also a hands-on look at a commercial data platform on a real task, not a demo.

Nimble designed the task. Each participant gets access to Nimble's data collection APIs for up to 72 hours, starting Tuesday, June 27, and uses them to solve it. The ten best performers are rewarded, with credits on Nimble's platform for all ten and an extra prize for first place. Sign-up goes through a form, and we hope many of you will take part.

## What's inside

- The 72 hours run non-stop from the June 27 kickoff, so timing and effort are part of the challenge.
- Nimble's data collection APIs are the tools provided to solve the task.
- All ten winners get $1,000 in Nimble credits, and the first-place winner also gets a $500 Amazon gift card.
- Participation is open to analysts, data scientists and curious tech people, not only seasoned scrapers.
