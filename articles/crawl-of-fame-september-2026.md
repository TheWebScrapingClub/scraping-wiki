# Crawl of Fame - September 2026

> Summary of an article published on [The Web Scraping Club](https://www.scraping.club/?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary) on 2026-09-08 (free to read). **[Read the full article](https://www.scraping.club/p/crawl-of-fame-september-2026?utm_source=wiki&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=article_summary)**

September starts the web scraping event season, and this edition of the Crawl of Fame has two dates to mark. Oxycon 2026 is free and online, with recordings only for registered attendees, and this year I'm on a panel instead of giving a talk. FuelTurin is the networking aperitivo I'm organizing with DataFuel in Turin during Wave by Vento week, for people working with web data and AI.

The third section follows up on my August 30 post about Google replacing destination URLs in the SERP with goto tokens. Commenters described setups where the wrappers show up differently than in my runs, so I compare their observations with mine. We also ran a resolution test on the tokens at volume, and I go through what DataForSEO and SerpApi announced, and what SISTRIX said about the cost of the workaround.

## What's inside

- My Oxycon panel is "The Adaptive Web", shared with speakers from Adobe, NielsenIQ and Limy AI, and I list the sessions I'm curious about, including loading dynamic content without a browser and whether the web is closing.
- FuelTurin takes place on October 7 on a rooftop in Turin, for around 40 founders, web data experts and AI builders, with registration by approval and no pitches, slides or demos.
- Commenters tried incognito windows, Firefox and signed-in profiles on Chrome and Edge, and I say what I make of the differences from my own runs.
- The resolution test looked at request method, concurrency and token lifetime, and the vendor section reports which SERP features DataForSEO says it still can't resolve.
